## 3. Latent Financial Stress Detector Using Non-Financial Signals

**Category:** Behavioral systems / early warning

Not compliance, not lending directly

### Core problem

Financial distress shows up before defaults—but outside financial systems.

Signals like:

- sudden HR attrition
- vendor churn
- IT service downgrades
- delayed internal approvals

Nobody correlates these.

### The idea

A system that:

- Ingests operational metadata (not money)
- Detects patterns of organizational stress
- Outputs a latent financial stress probability

This is a pre-financial risk layer.

### Why it’s untapped

Finance teams ignore operational exhaust.

### Research angle

- Early warning systems
- Cross-domain signal fusion
- Organizational stress modeling

## 4. Inter-Company Dependency Risk Graph (Supply-Chain but Financial)

**Category:** Graph systems / systemic risk

Not logistics. Not SCM software.

### Core problem

Companies fail because someone else they depend on fails.

But dependency is not just suppliers:

- payment processors
- credit providers
- platforms
- large customers

This risk is invisible.

### The idea

A graph-based system that:

- Maps financial and operational dependencies

Simulates:

- node failure
- liquidity shock
- platform outage

Outputs cascade risk scores

### Why it’s different

This is systemic risk at the enterprise level, not macroeconomics.

### Who cares

Banks, insurers, large enterprises, policymakers.
