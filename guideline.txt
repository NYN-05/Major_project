# **Guidelines for Major Project, Case Studies, Literature Review, and R&D

in AI-Driven FinTech Systems**

---

## **1. Project Scope and Domain Definition**

The major project shall be strictly confined to the **FinTech domain**, addressing real-world financial systems such as:

* Banking and NBFC operations
* Digital payments and UPI ecosystems
* Credit scoring and lending platforms
* Fraud detection and risk management
* Regulatory technology (RegTech)
* Financial inclusion and alternative finance

The solution **must leverage Artificial Intelligence**, including but not limited to:

* Machine Learning (ML)
* Deep Learning (DL)
* Reinforcement Learning (RL)
* Graph-based learning
* Explainable AI (XAI)
* Federated and privacy-preserving learning

Projects outside FinTech or those using AI only superficially shall be considered **out of scope**.

---

## **2. Core Project Eligibility Conditions**

To qualify as a valid major project, the idea **must satisfy all** of the following:

1. **Research Novelty**
   The idea must be demonstrably unique and suitable for submission to peer-reviewed journals.

2. **Research Gap Identification**
   The project must explicitly address an **unresolved or under-explored gap** identified through systematic literature review.

3. **Literature Backing**
   The problem formulation and methodology must be supported by a **substantial body of prior research**, including journals, conference papers, and industry reports.

4. **AI-Centric Methodology**
   AI/ML/DL must be the **core enabler**, not an add-on.

5. **Market Relevance**
   The solution must solve a **real, existing market problem** and be feasible for adoption by industry stakeholders.

6. **Market Gap Fulfilment**
   The project must clearly articulate **why existing solutions fail or are insufficient**, and how the proposed approach fills that gap.

---

## **3. Research-Grade Novelty Condition (Mandatory)**

The project must introduce **at least one** form of academic novelty:

* A **new problem formulation** specific to FinTech constraints
* A **new dataset** or data construction methodology
* A **new evaluation framework** aligned with financial risk or compliance
* A **new hybrid or composite model architecture**
* A **new contextual constraint**, such as India-specific regulation, infrastructure limitations, or behavioral patterns

> Replacing one algorithm with another without conceptual contribution does **not** qualify as novelty.

---

## **4. Data Credibility and Authenticity Condition**

The project must use **realistic and defensible data sources**, satisfying at least one of the following:

* Public real-world datasets (e.g., RBI, World Bank, NSE, Kaggle, UCI)
* Web-scraped or API-based data with a **clearly documented pipeline**
* Weakly labeled, incomplete, or noisy real-world data

Purely synthetic datasets without grounding in real data **weaken both publication and industry credibility**.

---

## **5. Regulatory, Ethical, and Trust Alignment**

Given the sensitivity of financial systems, the solution must explicitly address:

### **a. Fairness and Bias**

* Bias detection across demographic or behavioral groups
* Mitigation strategies where imbalance is identified

### **b. Explainability and Transparency**

* Model interpretability using SHAP, LIME, counterfactual explanations, or equivalent techniques
* Justifiable decision logic suitable for audits and regulatory scrutiny

### **c. Privacy and Security**

* Privacy-preserving learning (federated learning, differential privacy, anonymization)
* Compliance awareness with financial data protection norms

> FinTech systems without trust, transparency, and fairness lack both academic rigor and commercial viability.

---

## **6. Benchmarking and Evaluation Standards**

The proposed system must be rigorously evaluated against:

1. **Classical machine learning models**
2. **At least one deep learning architecture**
3. **At least one industry-standard or widely cited baseline**

Evaluation must include:

* Quantitative metrics
* Robustness under noisy or low-data scenarios
* Clear justification of performance gains

Projects without proper benchmarking are **not publishable**.

---

## **7. Market Adoption and Practical Viability Constraint**

The project must demonstrate:

* **Measurable reduction** in cost, risk, latency, or operational complexity
* **Feasibility of adoption** by banks, NBFCs, fintech startups, or regulators
* Functionality under **imperfect, sparse, or evolving data conditions**

Solutions that only work under ideal laboratory conditions lack real-world relevance.

---

## **8. Guidance for Literature Review and Case Studies**

The literature review shall:

* Identify **what has been done**, **what works**, and **what fails**
* Highlight **explicit research gaps**, not generic limitations
* Include both **academic** and **industry-driven** perspectives

Case studies shall:

* Reflect real FinTech workflows
* Validate assumptions under practical constraints
* Strengthen the market relevance of the proposed solution

---

## **9. Research and Development Roadmap**

The project development lifecycle should follow:

1. Problem formulation and gap identification
2. Data sourcing and preprocessing
3. Model design and experimentation
4. Benchmarking and evaluation
5. Explainability and compliance validation
6. Market feasibility and deployment considerations

Each stage must be **clearly documented** and justified.

---

## **10. Final Acceptance Criterion**

A project satisfying all the above guidelines shall be considered:

* **Academically publishable**
* **Industry-relevant**
* **Regulatory-aware**
* **Technically rigorous**
* **Market-viable**

Anything less is a weak project, regardless of code complexity.
